What Peanut is.

Peanut is a global cash app. Send money to anyone, pay like a local wherever you go. You probably remember us from Devconnect. We went a bit quiet to bring a big thing: a (very pink) platinum card, so you can go anywhere.

The Card Closed Beta.

The card is the first time a Peanut balance works anywhere, not just Brazil and Argentina. We're letting the early crew in first — if you were at Devconnect BA or you're an OG, you skip the waitlist. Early users get paid back for being early. Your job: go through the flow, grab the asset, post.

Get in

You qualify

Devconnect / OG badge holders skip the line straight to the "I'm in" moment.

Turned away

You don't

No badge → the Berghain-style "not tonight" door. Still yours to post — and funnier.

Either way

You post

Win or rejection, it's content. Celebrate, or rage. Both pull people in.

The hook every post carries: "Were you at Devconnect BA, or an OG? You can get the Peanut card right now — no waitlist." Tag @joinpeanut.

The flow.

1

Get your link

peanut.me/shhhhh — live now, you can open it today. Add a calendar reminder for Monday so you post when the whole launch fires together.

2

Sign up / log in

Open the link → Face ID / fingerprint sign-up, ~2 min. Returning? Just log into your old account.

3

Press & hold "see if you qualify"

The card flow puts up a pixelated card and a hold button — "we're looking you up." Hold it; we check your badges.

4

The reveal

Badge the "I'M IN!" asset lands with confetti. No badge the dark "not tonight, <you>" door. Both shown below.

5

Tap Share

Share (or Save image) fires the share sheet with the asset and a caption pre-filled. Rewrite the caption in your voice — the auto one is just a starting point.

6

Post on X

Tag @joinpeanut. Got in → celebrate. Turned away → flame, rage, beef. Pretend to be furious at the door. Drama is the point.

7

The reward

Early users get paid back for being early. We send it to you directly after launch — nothing to claim, no tab to check. It lands in your account.

What to post.

Tap Share and the asset posts with a caption already filled in. The asset is the image; the caption is the text that goes with it. Keep the @joinpeanut tag — rewrite the words in your own voice.
